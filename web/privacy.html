Privacy & terms

FloodAid provides contextual information, not emergency dispatch, verified road-clearance advice or guaranteed inventory. Measurements apply at their reported location and time. Missing reports do not establish safety.

Information on this GitHub Pages site

Public flood observations and place listings are downloaded from their named sources. Weather requests send the selected district coordinates to Open-Meteo. Route requests send selected start and destination coordinates to the FOSSGIS routing service. Your current location is accessed only when you request it. Shared report submission, account sign-in and account-saved destinations are not connected in this version.

Browser storage

The site stores interface preferences and cached public observations on your device. A Google Maps key entered through Map settings is stored only in this tab's session storage. It is not submitted to GitHub or to a FloodAid database. Clear it with “Use OpenStreetMap instead,” or clear your browser's site data.

Optional Google Maps

If you enable Google Maps, Google receives the browser key, map requests and information described in its privacy policy. A browser API key is visible in browser requests. Restrict it to your website and Maps JavaScript API. A site-wide key supplied by the repository owner is included in the deployed JavaScript, not kept secret by GitHub Actions.

Google Maps use is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Other providers

Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages. Map tiles and place data are from OpenStreetMap unless Google Maps is selected. Flood data is from BMA via ThaiWater. Routing is provided by FOSSGIS/OSRM. Each provider may process requests under its own policies. No private records from the earlier app have been copied to this public site.

Questions or corrections: FloodAid repository issues. Do not post private health, location or account information in public issues.

Worldwide reports and location

City searches are sent to Open-Meteo. Your chosen city or optional GPS coordinates and filters stay on this device. Map providers receive coordinates when you view maps or continue to a route or place search. Global reports come from USGS and NASA EONET; source coverage is incomplete, and distances do not indicate danger. Emergency shortcuts cover selected countries only.
